What this impact area is measured by

The plan indicator for Communications is the percentage of students and employees its outreach reaches, scored by the Association for the Advancement of Sustainability in Higher Education (AASHE) through its Sustainability Tracking, Assessment & Rating System (STARS); credit codes such as EN 1 refer to its Engagement credits. It is the indicator the master plan carries. Two supporting indicators sit beside it: the STARS outreach criteria, and the College's own measure of consistent reporting. Select one to open it. Where an indicator is not yet measured, the panel shows when it will be, never a placeholder number.

Where this stands today

Okanagan College is already building a community around sustainability. A monthly newsletter, shared through the College's Microsoft Teams channels, brings local news and events, sustainability updates and a monthly pledge, orientation days carry a sustainability campaign, and the College has reported its carbon emissions to the Province every year since 2008. The next step is to measure how far that work reaches and to add a yearly public account of the plan as a whole.

Where these numbers come from

The College's Current State record for Communications, last updated 22 October 2026, its public sustainability pages and newsletter archive read in October 2026, and its public STARS reports on the AASHE site. Nothing here is estimated. The newsletter count is the run shown on the public archive; the Current State record dates the start of the run two months earlier, and that difference is carried as an open question.

The next five months, on one line

The first STARS submission of the plan, the first reach measurement and the end of the College's current Reporter status all fall inside one winter. Most of Year 1 is set by these dates.

How this was worked out

The STARS position, verified against the 3.0.1 manual

Twenty eight points sit in this impact area: outreach and communications (5), co-curricular activities (9), community partnerships (9), shared facilities (2) and collaboration with other institutions (3). Every Engagement credit applies to every institution, so there is no route to remove any of them from the denominator. Three related credits are counted in other impact areas, one of them a bonus credit this area supplies the evidence for.

Credit by credit

The target, stated three times

An outcome in this area's own unit and a lever in a unit AASHE scores, as every module in this plan carries, then a third card for the public engagement credits, which carry more points than the outreach credit. The third card is a deliberate deviation from the two card pattern.

Three options, differing in how far the College reports beyond what is required

The detail behind each option

Three other axes were tested and set aside. Dependence on outside money does not separate options here, because the spending is almost entirely staff time. Dependence on other parties is real, since college communications holds the main channels, but it applies equally to all three options. Campus coverage separates options only through staffing, which belongs to the Organizational Structure and Funding impact area, so it sits inside the recommended option as a requirement instead of defining the options.
